How to apply for BIS recognition

To apply for BIS recognition, please visit lims.bis.gov.in, select ‘Login’ and choose the option ‘New Lab/Register Now’.The User Manual for providing guidance for filling up the application of LIMS portal is also available at lims.bis.gov.in.For any queries, please write to:Head,
